1.What is Data Science ?
Data science is the process of using data/information, programming, statistics and domain knowledge to answer questions, discover patterns, make predictions and support decisions.

#Data science pipeline: 

1. Understand the problem
        ↓
2. Obtain the data
        ↓
3. Clean the data
        ↓
4. Explore the data
        ↓
5. Visualize it
        ↓
6. Find patterns
        ↓
7. Form hypotheses
        ↓
8. Build statistical/ML models
        ↓
9. Evaluate them
        ↓
10. Interpret the results
        ↓
11. Communicate the findings

#Difference b/w Data Science v/s Data Analysis v/s Machine Learning
*Data analysis is used to know what happened
*Data science helps to understand what happened, why it might have happened, and what can we predict or learn from the data? by combining analysis, statistics, programming, domain knowledge and often machine learning.
*Machine learning helps by learning patterns from existing data and use it to make predictions on new data.
